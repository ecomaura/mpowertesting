About Us

A Bavarian supplier of fixing systems for construction and industry

is based in Neutraubling and supplies fixing systems to contractors, fabricators and distributors working across cladding, roofing and structural steel. The product range spans self-drilling and self-tapping screws, rivets and saddle washers for building envelope work, structural bolt assemblies and shear connectors for steel frames, and the general hardware that construction and fabrication sites depend on day to day.

We work directly with site teams and procurement contacts rather than through a layered sales process. If a specification needs checking against a DIN or EN standard before an order goes out, that conversation happens with someone who knows the product.

Quality Assurance

Quality Control and Testing

Every fastener line goes through the same testing path before a batch is released, not just a spot check on the finished part.

Tensile & Hardness Testing Mechanical Properties

Fasteners are tested for tensile strength and hardness to confirm they meet the stated grade before a batch is released.

Chemical Composition Material Verification

Material composition is checked against the specified steel or stainless grade before production begins.

Coating & Salt Spray Corrosion Resistance

Ruspert and zinc coatings are salt spray tested to confirm corrosion resistance holds up under sustained exposure.

Dimensional & Visual Final Inspection

Thread, head and drill point dimensions are checked against drawing tolerances, with a visual pass for finish defects.
